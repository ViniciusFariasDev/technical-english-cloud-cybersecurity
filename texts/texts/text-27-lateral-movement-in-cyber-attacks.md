# Text 27 – Technical English – Lateral Movement in Cyber Attacks

## 📄 Text

Lateral movement  
is a technique used by attackers  
to move through a network  
after gaining initial access.

Instead of attacking systems directly,  
attackers explore the network  
to find valuable targets,  
such as servers,  
databases,  
or administrator accounts.

They may use stolen credentials,  
remote access tools,  
or system vulnerabilities  
to move between systems.

Lateral movement allows attackers  
to expand their access,  
avoid detection,  
and increase the impact  
of the attack.

Security teams monitor network traffic,  
user behavior,  
and system logs  
to detect lateral movement  
and stop attackers early.

---

## 📚 Vocabulary

- **Lateral movement** – movimentação lateral  
- **Initial access** – acesso inicial  
- **Credentials** – credenciais  
- **Remote access** – acesso remoto  
- **Administrator accounts** – contas administrativas  
- **Detection** – detecção  
- **Network traffic** – tráfego de rede
