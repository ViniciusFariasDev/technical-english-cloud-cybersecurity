# Text 26 – Technical English – Privilege Escalation in Cybersecurity

## 📄 Text

Privilege escalation  
is an attack technique  
where an attacker gains  
higher access permissions  
than originally allowed.

Attackers may exploit vulnerabilities,  
misconfigurations,  
or weak access controls  
to escalate privileges.  
This allows them  
to access restricted systems,  
sensitive data,  
or administrative functions.

There are two main types  
of privilege escalation:  
vertical escalation,  
where attackers gain administrator access,  
and horizontal escalation,  
where attackers access  
other user accounts.

Security teams use monitoring tools,  
access controls,  
and vulnerability management  
to prevent privilege escalation.

Preventing unauthorized privilege access  
is critical  
for protecting systems  
and maintaining security.

---

## 📚 Vocabulary

- **Privilege escalation** – escalonamento de privilégios  
- **Permissions** – permissões  
- **Exploit** – explorar  
- **Misconfigurations** – configurações incorretas  
- **Restricted systems** – sistemas restritos  
- **Administrative functions** – funções administrativas  
- **Unauthorized** – não autorizado
